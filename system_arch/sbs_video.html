Side by Side Video

To provide situational awareness, the system features a dedicated video feedback loop. A camera (IMX 708) mounted on the rover captures live video. This video stream is transmitted to a Rubik Pi 3 located on the user's headset, which formats the feed into a stereoscopic Side-by-Side display for the AR headset. This decoupled architecture ensures the video pipeline does not interrupt the core FPGA inference tasks.
